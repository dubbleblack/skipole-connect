## Short announcement

**Skipole — licensed GTM audiences for your tools and AI workflows**

Your tools. Our data. Find it. Pay for it. Plug it in. Repeat.

Skipole is a self-service marketplace for licensed B2B contact audiences. Choose
Marketing, Sales, Engineering, Finance, Human Resources or Operations, review the
fields and price, approve hosted Checkout, and retrieve your CSV through My
Orders or authenticated REST. These are six configurations of one audience engine.

PAYG starts at $0.05 per delivered record with a $2 minimum Checkout, before
applicable taxes. No subscription or sales call is required. Optional Builder
and Scale memberships offer lower record rates; no records are included.

For developers: JavaScript and Python packages are published, the MCP server is
listed, and signed quote links keep the human in control of payment. Paid REST
purchasing and delivery were verified in Production with an internal purchase;
paid native SDK/MCP purchases are not independently verified.

The licensed fields are first name, last name, business email and LinkedIn URL.
There are no phone or title fields, email-deliverability guarantees or automatic
resale rights. Review the licence and use your own compliance controls.

Try it: https://skipoledata.com/marketplace
